How to use Prism Travels’ information and enquiry website.

The Prism Travels website helps you discover travel services and start a conversation. It does not itself issue tickets or confirm bookings.

1. Website purpose

Destination descriptions, service information and images are provided for general inspiration. They are not binding offers, quotations, booking confirmations or guarantees of availability.

2. Travel enquiries

Submitting an enquiry through the embedded Google Form does not book travel or create a payment obligation. Before arranging travel, confirm the proposed itinerary, prices, schedule, inclusions, cancellation conditions and any supplier-specific terms directly with the team.

3. Current travel requirements

Flights, visas, entry rules, hotels and other travel conditions may change. Check the latest official rules and your final booking documents before travel. Government bodies and third-party suppliers are responsible for their own decisions and services.

4. External services

This website embeds an external enquiry service (Google Forms) and loads third-party images and fonts. Those services remain subject to their own terms and policies.

5. Intellectual property

Prism branding and original website text are intended for Prism Travels. Third-party destination images remain the property of their respective rights holders and are used subject to applicable licences.

6. Queries about bookings

If you need transaction-specific details or have a question about a quoted itinerary, ask for the applicable written terms before proceeding. This website's general terms do not replace any separate booking agreement.
