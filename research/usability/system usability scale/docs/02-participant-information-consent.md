## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to take part in a usability study of the TRIP by PRISM public website, operated by PRISM Travel and Advisory Limited. We are studying how clearly the site explains its services and how easily people can find travel information or a suitable contact route. We are **testing the website, not your ability**. Difficulty finding something is useful information for us.

## What participation involves

A researcher will present up to six realistic travel-planning tasks while you use the website. You may be asked to describe your thoughts aloud as you browse. An observer may note actions, comments, time spent and whether you needed help. After the tasks you will complete a short ten-question System Usability Scale (SUS) survey and optional feedback questions. The full session is expected to take about **40–50 minutes**. You will not be required to make a booking, provide payment information or share passport/identity details.

## Your choice and right to withdraw

Participation is completely voluntary. You may skip any question, pause or stop at any time without providing a reason or affecting your relationship with PRISM. Where a participant code still links to your data, you may request withdrawal of your responses up to **[WITHDRAWAL_DEADLINE, e.g. seven calendar days after your session]** using your code and emailing `[RESEARCH_EMAIL]`. After records have been irreversibly combined or de-identified, individual removal may no longer be possible; the research team will tell you when that applies. If you decline, no study session will begin.

## What is recorded and how it is protected

Unless separately agreed, researchers will take written notes only; **audio, video and screen recording are OFF by default**. The forms request a participant code rather than a name. Consent confirmation and usability responses are kept in separate, restricted Google files. Pseudonymised notes may still contain identifying details in quotations; the team will remove identifying content from any report or GitHub summary. Access is limited to the research team. Raw data will be kept for **[RETENTION_PERIOD]**, then securely removed unless the organisation has an approved reason to retain it longer. Google Forms/Sheets processing is subject to the chosen organisation's Google Workspace configuration and Google's service terms; the team should verify its data handling before inviting participants.

If a quote may be published, identifying context will be removed and the quote used only for the purpose explained here. No raw responses, names or recordings will be published on GitHub.

## Facilitator's consent procedure

1. Give the information above before starting. Invite questions privately.
2. Confirm adult status and voluntary participation; **do not start if the person declines**.
3. Assign a non-identifying participant code. Avoid taking a signature/name unless the legal/research owner requires it.
4. Have the participant submit the **separate consent Google Form**. Recording requires *separate affirmative consent*; default off.
5. Store any withdrawal-code mapping outside GitHub and separately from scored answers; tell the participant their code and contact method.
6. If consent is withdrawn, locate linked records by code and remove them if still identifiable; document the action in a private audit log.
