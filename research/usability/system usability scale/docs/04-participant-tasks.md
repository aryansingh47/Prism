# 04 · Participant Task Sheet — PRISM

**Study PRISM-UX-001** · Participant code: `P___` · Website: `tripbyprism.co.in`

## Before you begin

Imagine you are considering a travel agency to help plan a trip. Please use the website **as you normally would**, and talk through what you expect to happen, what you notice, and anything that feels unclear. There is no right way to navigate; please do not feel you need to impress us. If something is not obvious, tell us. You are free to stop or skip any task.

**Safety:** Do not enter real personal information, identity documents, payment information, usernames, passwords or traveller names. Do not submit a real booking, enquiry or chat containing personal details. When you reach a point where you *could* send an enquiry, tell the facilitator and stop before submitting.

The tasks describe **what you want to achieve**, not which page, menu or button to use.

---

### T01 — Discover what PRISM offers

> You have heard about TRIP by PRISM but do not know much about it. Explore the website briefly, then tell us which kinds of travel services you think the business provides and who they are for. Identify **at least two distinct services** using information you found on the site.

**Say when:** You are confident you can explain the business offering, or you cannot find enough information.

### T02 — Look for a leisure holiday option

> You are considering a short personal holiday and would prefer help organising the trip. Use the site to find a relevant holiday/package option **or** discover how you could request a tailored holiday. Explain what information you would check before proceeding.

**Say when:** You have found a useful option or an understandable next step, or you would give up.

### T03 — Find the next step for a flight and hotel enquiry

> You would like a flight and a hotel for an upcoming domestic trip. Find the route you would use to ask PRISM for assistance. Explain what information the website appears to need from you. **Stop before sending anything.**

**Say when:** You have reached a suitable enquiry/contact step without submitting it, or cannot find one.

### T04 — Explore corporate travel support

> You are arranging travel for **eight colleagues** who need transport and accommodation for a work event. Find out whether PRISM appears to offer corporate/group travel assistance, and where you would start an enquiry.

**Say when:** You can point out an appropriate service and next step, or cannot find one.

### T05 — Find a way to get help

> You have a question about an itinerary and prefer speaking with an actual person before committing. Find a way to contact PRISM. Tell us which channel you would use and why. **Do not send a real message.**

**Say when:** You have a contact route and can describe how it works, or cannot find one.

### T06 — Plan a multi-service travel request

> Imagine you want PRISM to help arrange a trip that includes **flights, accommodation and transport between the airport and the hotel**. Using what is available on the website, show or explain how you would make such a request and what you would expect to happen next. No submission is required.

**Say when:** You can clearly explain a plausible next step, or you cannot.

---

## After the six tasks

The facilitator will give you a short form with **10 statements about your experience of the PRISM website**. Please complete it based on the whole website experience, independently, with your first impression. You will then have an opportunity to share comments and suggestions.

Thank you for helping improve the site.
