# 05 · Facilitator Script and Run of Show

**Use verbatim as far as possible.** Do not reveal desired navigation, where buttons are, or the outcome of a task. For task text, read `04-participant-tasks.md` exactly; avoid supplementary hints that could bias results.

## Before the participant arrives

- Confirm agreed website release/commit and test URL; record environment in private session metadata.
- Confirm consent form works and survey link points to the correct private Google Form (not an example).
- Disable real bookings/payments, remove other customers' information, log out of staff areas, and clear residual forms or SIA chats.
- Prepare timer, observer sheet and the participant task cards; do not display observer success criteria.
- If remote: clarify screen sharing and stop recording unless separately authorised.

## 00:00–00:05 · Welcome and consent

**Read:**

> Thanks for taking part. We are evaluating how easily people can understand and use PRISM's travel website. The website is the subject of the test, not you. If something doesn't work or makes no sense, that's useful feedback. We have six short scenarios followed by a short questionnaire. This usually takes around 40 to 50 minutes.
>
> You can skip any question or stop at any point. Please avoid any real payment, passport, login or personal travel details. We won't complete any real booking or enquiry. With your permission, we will take notes about navigation and timing; recording is off unless we explicitly agree otherwise.
>
> Please read the information sheet, ask any questions and confirm whether you wish to take part.

**Action:** Check adult status, consent submission and participant code before proceeding. No participation if consent not given.

## 00:05–00:07 · Think-aloud orientation

**Read:**

> As you work through each task, tell us what you're looking for, what you expect a link or button to do, and what makes you hesitate. I may occasionally ask what you're thinking, but I won't tell you how to use the website. You can always say you'd stop or try something else.

**Do not** demonstrate site features or offer a training video. If participant asks “Am I doing this right?”, reply:

> There isn't a right or wrong approach here. Please try what feels natural to you.

## 00:07–00:36 · Core task sequence

For each **T01–T06**, follow the same sequence:

1. Reset to the agreed starting point (homepage, unless a task explicitly continues a journey).
2. Ask participant to read the task, or read the exact card aloud. Start stopwatch **after** they indicate they are ready.
3. Observe silently; if they stop speaking, prompt only: “What are you thinking now?”
4. If they request instructions, say once: “What would you try if you were doing this alone?”
5. If still blocked, log a standard neutral hint (**H1**); only give more explicit assistance if required to move on (**H2**). Record the exact words and mark the outcome as assisted; never mark as unaided afterward.
6. Stop on task end condition or task cap; ask: “Is that what you would do on your own?” and log confidence, but do **not** collect numeric ratings yet.
7. Briefly reset to homepage and advance. Do not discuss the design before the SUS.

**Neutral probes allowed during the task:**
- “What were you expecting to happen there?”
- “What would you try next?”
- “What does that wording mean to you?”
- “Could you describe what you're seeing?”

**Avoid leading prompts:**
- “Can you see the Packages menu?”
- “Did you notice the orange button?”
- “Don't you think the chat is useful?”
- “Click the contact form now.”

**Time caps:** T01 3m, T02 4m, T03 4m, T04 4m, T05 3m, T06 5m. Pilot these. A participant may stop early. Never force them to continue after distress, objection, or withdrawal.

## 00:36–00:42 · SUS assessment, privately and independently

**Read:**

> Please now complete the ten statements about the website you have just used. For these statements, “system” means the PRISM public website. Choose one response from 1, strongly disagree, to 5, strongly agree, for every statement. Please answer from your own overall impression. I'll give you privacy to complete the form and will not explain or rephrase the statements.

Provide post-session Google Form with matching code; observer/facilitator steps away. **Do not** show prior participant results or prompt for a high score. Questions S01–S10 must remain in their original order and wording.

## 00:42–00:49 · Debrief (after SUS submission)

**Read:**

> Thank you. I'd like to understand your experience in your own words. What was easiest? What was most frustrating or uncertain? Which one thing should we improve first? Did anything make you hesitate about contacting PRISM or using the site in a real travel situation? Anything else you expected that you couldn't find?

Ask non-leading follow-ups. If participant raises a bug or missing feature, gather exact route and reproduction details without suggesting a solution. Optional SIA module may follow as an expressly separate exploration, after the core SUS.

## Close

**Read:**

> That's everything for this session. Thank you for your feedback. If you decide you want your responses withdrawn within the period explained in the participant information sheet, you can contact the research team using your participant code. Your participation and views won't be attributed to you in any public report.

## Facilitator deviation log

Record deviations privately in session metadata: session ID, task ID, exact hint wording, website unavailable, participant stopped, mistaken task text, test-environment disruption, or recording consent changes. **Never** silently overwrite original measurements.
