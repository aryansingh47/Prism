# 03 · Recruitment, Screening and Scheduling

## Participant profile

- Adults (18+) who might use a travel agency website for leisure, group or business travel.
- At least some first-time PRISM visitors, some frequent online trip planners, and some less digitally confident planners.
- Include people outside the product team. Anyone who built, wrote or tested this exact PRISM interface should be flagged as **expert/internal** and analysed separately or excluded from the novice round.
- Include mobile users in a separate or labelled device cohort; avoid comparing runs on materially different versions.

## Suggested sampling grid (not statistical quotas)

| Trait | Suggested coverage | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel intention | Leisure, business/group, mixed | Different navigation goals |
| Booking confidence | Low, medium, high | Learnability and terminology |
| Familiarity with PRISM | Mostly none | Genuine first encounter |
| Browser/device | Defined desktop round; optional mobile round | Consistent baseline |
| Accessibility needs | Offer accommodations | Identify navigation/readability obstacles |

**Goal:** 12–16 adult participants if feasible, plus 1–2 pilot sessions. For a rapid diagnostic round, 5–8 can expose issues but will produce a less stable mean SUS. Report actual N and recruitment sources.

## Pre-session screener (keep scheduling data separate)

1. Are you 18 years or older? `[Yes / No]` — only adults eligible in this protocol.
2. Have you visited TRIP by PRISM before? `[Never / Once or twice / Often]`.
3. Which best describes your travel planning? `[Mostly leisure / Mostly work or corporate / Both / Neither]`.
4. How often do you research or book trips online? `[Rarely / Sometimes / Frequently]`.
5. Which device do you most commonly use for travel planning? `[Phone / Laptop or desktop / Tablet / No strong preference]`.
6. Have you been directly involved in designing or developing PRISM's website? `[Yes / No]`.
7. Would you prefer any accessibility accommodation for the session? `[Optional free text — do not ask for diagnosis]`.
8. Are you available on `[dates]`? `[availability only; store privately]`.

Do not collect gender, precise age, address, passport numbers or health details unless a documented, approved research question requires them. Store names/contact details for recruitment separately from participant observations.

## Allocation and participant IDs

Allocate `P001`, `P002` and so on. If more than one variant/device group: maintain a **private** allocation file with the code, invitation contact, session schedule and consent status. Do not put this file in GitHub. The public sample CSV templates contain only ID fields, no people's data.

## Researcher readiness

Before recruitment, finalise the privacy contact, retention schedule, moderator(s), pilot build, testing URL, optional incentive and procedure for screen-sharing consent. Never promise that data is "anonymous" if an ID lookup or raw video can identify someone; use "pseudonymised" until de-identification is complete.
