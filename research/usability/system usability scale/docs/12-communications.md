## Invitation email

**Subject:** Invitation: Help us improve the TRIP by PRISM website

Hello,

PRISM Travel and Advisory Limited is inviting volunteers to take part in a short usability study of our website. We're interested in how easily people can find information about travel services and how intuitive the enquiry process feels.

The session lasts approximately **40–50 minutes** and involves trying six travel-related scenarios, sharing your first impressions, and completing a short questionnaire. We are evaluating the website, not your skills. No bookings, payments, identity documents or real travel enquiries will be required.

Participation is voluntary. If you'd like to take part, please reply to `[RESEARCH_EMAIL]` or use `[APPROVED_SCHEDULING_LINK]`. We will send you the participant information sheet before your session.

Thank you,  
`[Research lead]`
