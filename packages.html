Walk through Rajasthan’s royal cities, from Jaipur’s ornate facades to Jodhpur’s fort and Udaipur’s lakeside charm.

Jaipur → Jodhpur → Udaipur

FORTS · PALACES · LAKES
See the complete 6-day itinerary⌄

    Arrival in Jaipur

    Arrive Jaipur, hotel check-in, Hawa Mahal exterior, City Palace area and local market.

  2. Day 2

    The Pink City

    Amber Fort, Jal Mahal photo stop, Jantar Mantar and Jaipur market.

  3. Day 3

    Jaipur to Jodhpur

    Drive Jaipur–Jodhpur (about 5–6 hours); visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada, time permitting.

  4. Day 4

    Jodhpur to Udaipur

    Drive Jodhpur–Udaipur (about 5–6 hours); check in and enjoy Lake Pichola/old city.

  5. Day 5

    Udaipur’s highlights

    City Palace, Saheliyon-ki-Bari and lakefront; overnight Udaipur.

  6. Day 6

    Departure

    Udaipur airport/railway station drop.
