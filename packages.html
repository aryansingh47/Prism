THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

What your estimate covers.

We believe a good trip starts with clear expectations. Here's what to know before confirming.

Common proposed inclusions

  • Accommodation for the stated nights
  • Daily breakfast
  • Ground transportation and sightseeing transfers described in your selected itinerary
  • Driver allowances and tolls/parking where specified

Common exclusions

  • Air and train tickets
  • Lunch and dinner
  • GST unless explicitly included in the final quote
  • Entry fees, activities and optional local/union vehicles
  • Personal expenses and travel insurance
