A plain-language overview of how enquiries and personal information work on this website.

We respect the importance of your personal travel details. This page explains how this particular website operates and where information may be shared.

1. Information you provide

Prism Travels provides a first-party travel enquiry form on its Contact page. When you submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, contact details, travel dates, destination, preferences and other information requested by the form. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal documents unless specifically required through a verified process.

2. How the enquiry information is used

Information submitted through the enquiry form is intended to help the travel team understand your request, discuss options and follow up on your enquiry. Contact the team through the form if you have questions about the use, correction or deletion of information previously supplied.

3. Registered accounts and enquiry history

When you choose to create an account, we store your name, email address and a one-way password hash. Session cookies are used to keep you signed in. Guest enquiries do not require registration. Signed-in customers may review their own submitted enquiries.

4. Third-party tools and resources

This website uses Google Fonts and loads travel photography from Unsplash and Pexels. Enquiries and account information are stored in a database operated by our hosting provider. An email service may be used for account verification, password resets and enquiry notifications. These providers may receive technical connection data and have their own privacy terms. You can read Google's privacy policy and Unsplash's privacy information.

5. Session cookies, security and analytics

This website uses essential session cookies for authentication and protection against fraudulent submissions. It does not set advertising cookies through the included Prism code. Security rate-limits process limited connection information. External fonts and imagery may receive ordinary connection data.

6. Access, correction, retention and questions

To raise a privacy-related question, contact Prism through the enquiry form and clearly state that the question concerns your information.
