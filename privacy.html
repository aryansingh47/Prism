A plain-language overview of how enquiries and personal information work on this website.

We respect the importance of your personal travel details. This page explains how this particular website operates and where information may be shared.

1. Information you provide

Prism Travels displays a Google Form within this website when you choose to make an enquiry. When you submit that form, you may provide your name, contact details, travel dates, destination, preferences and other information requested by the form. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal documents unless specifically required through a verified process.

2. How the enquiry information is used

Information submitted through the enquiry form is intended to help the travel team understand your request, discuss options and follow up on your enquiry. Contact the team through the form if you have questions about the use, correction or deletion of information previously supplied.

3. The on-page trip brief

The optional trip brief generator on the contact page runs in your browser. It prepares text for you to copy and does not send information to a Prism server. Clipboard permissions depend on your browser settings.

4. Third-party tools and resources

This website uses Google Fonts and loads travel photography from Unsplash and Pexels. The enquiry dialog embeds Google Forms when opened. These providers may receive technical connection data and have their own privacy terms. You can read Google's privacy policy and Unsplash's privacy information.

5. Cookies and analytics

The provided website code does not install Prism-owned analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies. External providers may process technical data as described in their policies. Any future addition of analytics or marketing services should be reflected in an updated privacy notice.

6. Questions

To raise a privacy-related question, contact Prism through the enquiry form and clearly state that the question concerns your information.
