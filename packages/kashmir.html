GARDENS · MOUNTAINS · VALLEYS

  1. Day 1

    Welcome to Srinagar

    Srinagar airport pickup, check in and leisure; optional shikara ride (extra unless added).

  2. Day 2

    Discover Srinagar

    Mughal Gardens, Dal Lake area and old city, as accessible.

  3. Day 3

    Gulmarg’s mountains

    Drive Srinagar–Gulmarg; gondola ride is optional and subject to ticket/operations; overnight Gulmarg.

  4. Day 4

    On to Pahalgam

    Drive Gulmarg–Pahalgam; check in and leisure.

  5. Day 5

    Pahalgam’s valleys

    Pahalgam local valleys/sightseeing; restricted-route local union vehicles and pony rides extra.

  6. Day 6

    Departure

    Drive to Srinagar airport for departure.
