Frequently asked questions.

A few useful details before you get started.

Ask Prism a question ↗
Can I discuss a trip in person?

Yes. Prism offers a person-led way to plan travel. Request the current office location and appointment arrangements before visiting.

Do you only plan international travel?

No. You can enquire about journeys within India as well as international travel.

Can you organise travel for a group or a company?

Yes. We welcome enquiries about corporate travel, group itineraries, events and destination celebrations. Share your group size and requirements.

Are prices and availability shown on this website?

No. This is an information and enquiry website, not a live booking engine. Prices and availability are discussed and confirmed separately.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. It starts a conversation. A booking is only made after the relevant details, availability, pricing and terms have been confirmed.

Can you guarantee a visa or entry approval?

No. Official requirements and approval decisions remain with the issuing authorities. Please consult government sources for current rules.
